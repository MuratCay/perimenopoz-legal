The calm, private tracker for perimenopause and HRT.

Support

Questions, problems or suggestions: dev.murat.cay@gmail.com. We usually reply within a few days.

When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model and iOS version. Never include your health entries in an email; we do not need them and cannot see them.

Your data

  • Everything you log stays on your iPhone. There is no account and no server of ours.
  • You can erase everything at any time in the app: Settings → Delete All Data.
  • This app is not a medical device. It is a log of what you enter; discuss your symptoms and any changes with your doctor.

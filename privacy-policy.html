Perimenopoz Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Perimenopoz ("the app") is a general wellness log for perimenopause and HRT. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app stores, where it is stored, and what, if anything, leaves your device.

The short version

  • The health information you log is stored only on your iPhone. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • No account. You never sign up or log in.
  • No servers of ours. We do not run a backend and cannot see your data.
  • No ads, no advertising SDKs, no tracking, and no third-party analytics of your health data.
  • We never sell, rent or share your data.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Murat Çay, an individual developer. Contact: dev.murat.cay@gmail.com.

Information you enter (stays on your device)

The app lets you record hot flashes and night sweats, a daily symptom check-in, period dates, medications (including HRT) with their doses, dose changes and doses taken, lab results, and notes. It also keeps a few settings, such as your reminder times.

This is health information. It is saved only in the app's own storage on your iPhone, shared with the app's home screen widget on the same device. It is not uploaded to any server and it is not synced to iCloud. We use it only on your device, to show your calendar, trends and the reports you ask for.

Your consent

If you are in the UK, the European Economic Area or a region with similar rules, health information is a special category of personal data. Before you start logging, the app asks for your explicit consent to store this information on your device. The consent is versioned: if what the app stores changes in a way that matters, the app asks again. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting all data (see below).

Information that leaves your device

Only when you choose to export it. Your data leaves the app in a file only when you ask for one:

  • Doctor report (PDF). Created on your device; it goes only where you send it.
  • Backup file. Created on your device when you ask for it and encrypted with a passphrase you choose. We never see the passphrase and cannot recover it or the file's contents. You decide where the file is kept (for example the Files app, AirDrop or email). You can import it again on your own device.

Subscriptions. Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details. To check whether you have Premium, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-validation service. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID created by the app, your purchase and subscription details, and basic technical information (such as app version, device model, iOS version and App Store country). It does not receive your name, email, Apple Account, or any health information you have logged. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Notifications

Reminders (for example for a daily check-in or a medication) are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. They are not sent through any server. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account, and we do not collect your name or email through the app.
  • We do not show ads, and we do not sell or share your information for advertising.
  • We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites.
  • We do not send your health information to analytics, advertising or any other third party.

Deleting your data

You can erase everything the app has stored by going to Settings → Delete All Data. Deleting the app also removes its data from your iPhone. Because we hold none of your logs, there is nothing for us to delete on a server. Backup files you exported are under your control; delete them wherever you saved them. Subscription records are held by Apple and RevenueCat and are managed through your Apple Account.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because the app keeps your health logs only on your device, you control them directly. For anything else, contact dev.murat.cay@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

The app is a general wellness log and is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or suggest doses. Discuss your symptoms and any treatment with your doctor.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. For material changes we will also tell you in the app.
